# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web application, faithfully adapting the user's existing mobile Figma prototype. Desktop retains its centered phone composition; mobile uses available width.

## Stack
Explicitly requested: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. App Router with static export. Lucide icons, Recharts interactive charts, Motion animations, Radix Themes buttons/switches, Radix Dialog, locally served IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Sans Thai.

## Users and purpose
University students checking suspicious scholarship, tuition and impersonation messages before acting.

## Capabilities
Text input; real local image selection, validation, drag/drop and preview; simulated analysis and loading; animated mock risk scores; related university references; session history; locally persisted settings; simulated phone alert; weekly report dashboard and scam-pattern guides.

## Evidence and constraints
All risk scores, evidence, message examples and statistics are mock. No AI/OCR, SMS/email ingestion, university API, or system-level emergency notification service exists. No image upload leaves the browser. Uploaded image previews stay in memory until the visit ends; only settings are persisted. Public university source links are contextual information, not verification of a sender.

## Voice and brand
Concise Thai copy. Follow current Figma, including greeting สวัสดี, เจมส์ and green empty-risk state. Pink #D9577D, white, deep pink #A72F55 for accessible actions. Font IBM Plex Sans with Thai companion.
